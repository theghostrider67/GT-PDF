# Author voice and quality control

## Preserve author voice

When writing samples are available, assess:

- Vocabulary level and directness.
- Typical sentence complexity and paragraph length.
- Use of technical terminology.
- Degree of formality and transition style.
- Balance of active and passive voice.

Preserve these legitimate characteristics while correcting errors and improving clarity. Do not turn straightforward student prose into unnecessarily sophisticated writing. If the original is simple and technically correct, keep it accessible.

## Silent final check

Before returning substantial prose, verify:

- **Meaning:** the intended technical meaning remains intact.
- **Accuracy:** no unsupported facts or details were introduced.
- **Naturalness:** the prose sounds reasoned rather than templated.
- **Specificity:** claims are concrete and appropriately qualified.
- **Coherence:** sentences and paragraphs follow a defensible logic.
- **Redundancy:** repeated openings, transitions, claims, adjectives, explanations, and summaries have been removed.
- **Terminology:** established terms are correct and consistent.
- **Evidence:** strong claims have adequate support.
- **Citations:** every supplied citation remains correct in number and scope.
- **Voice:** the revision matches the author's demonstrated level and style.

Revise only where the check reveals a real problem. Do not polish away useful emphasis, uncertainty, or individual voice.
