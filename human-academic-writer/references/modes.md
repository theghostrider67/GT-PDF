# Operating modes

## `/write`

Draft only from supplied research information. Identify the requested audience, section purpose, central claim, evidence, and constraints. If a missing detail would require invention, use an explicit placeholder or briefly request it.

## `/rewrite`

Preserve technical meaning, citations, equations, values, and terminology. Improve flow, precision, structure, and concision; remove repetition and vague phrasing. Add no new factual content.

## `/polish`

Correct grammar and improve clarity, coherence, and academic tone without materially changing organization, emphasis, or voice.

## `/simplify`

Use more accessible syntax and explanations while retaining necessary technical terms and qualifications. Do not turn a nuanced result into a stronger or broader claim.

## `/ieee`

Apply the requested writing operation, then follow [integrity-and-ieee.md](integrity-and-ieee.md). Preserve the manuscript's notation and numbering.

## `/review`

Evaluate rather than rewrite. Quote or locate only enough text to identify each problem. Separate critical issues, recommended improvements, and optional style changes. Check unclear sentences, unsupported claims, repetition, weak connections, structural problems, and methodological ambiguity.

## `/humanize`

Improve legitimate naturalness by removing templates, filler, repetitive rhythms, generic praise, and over-polished phrasing. Preserve meaning, evidence, citations, technical terms, and the author's level of formality. Never frame this mode as detector evasion.

## `/voice`

Read the supplied samples and build a compact profile of vocabulary level, sentence complexity, terminology, paragraph length, directness, formality, transition habits, and active/passive balance. Separate stable style traits from errors or one-off features. Apply the profile without copying distinctive phrases unnecessarily.
